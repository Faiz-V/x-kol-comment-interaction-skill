# Editorial angle library

Use only the section relevant to the source. Treat these as lenses, not facts to insert.

## AI and technology

- Product launch versus real workflow adoption
- Safety versus iteration speed
- Voluntary standards becoming de facto rules
- Open-source access and effects on smaller teams
- Distribution, latency, pricing, and developer migration
- Technical milestone versus policy signal

Natural patterns:

```text
The benchmark gets attention, but the adoption test is...
```

```text
Voluntary standards can become the rulebook before regulation catches up.
```

```text
The hard balance is improving safety without making experimentation impossible.
```

## Crypto, DeFi, and stablecoins

- Price action versus sustained on-chain activity
- Liquidity and positioning versus fundamentals
- User growth versus short-term speculation
- Stablecoin payment rails and practical adoption
- Custody, regulation, and institutional constraints
- Headline number versus follow-through

Natural patterns:

```text
Assuming the figures are right, the usage signal matters more than the price spike.
```

```text
Bullish headline, but the follow-through will tell us whether the behavior changed.
```

Never promise a pump, a return, or a guaranteed market outcome.

## Macro, FX, and equities

- Currency move versus household or policy pressure
- Mechanical flows versus a fundamentals reset
- Rate differentials and imported inflation
- Crowded positioning and reversal risk
- Market repricing versus narrative overreaction

Natural patterns:

```text
This is where FX stops being just a market move and starts becoming a policy problem.
```

```text
It looks less like a clean fundamentals reset and more like a flow-driven move.
```

Use causal language only when the source or verified evidence supports it.

## Football

- Pressure and knockout-stage impact
- Tactical fit, transitions, set pieces, and squad depth
- Late-game concentration and match management
- Reputation versus performance in decisive moments
- Fan emotion without attacking players or inventing motives

Natural patterns:

```text
You can control most of the match, but one lapse can still decide a knockout tie.
```

```text
The result gets the headline; the tactical adjustment changed the game.
```

Treat mental health, injury, and private life with extra restraint. Do not diagnose or speculate.

## General news and community discussion

- What changed versus what merely received attention
- Direct effect versus broader sentiment backdrop
- First-order benefit versus second-order trade-off
- Headline claim versus the evidence needed to sustain it
- Institutional view versus user experience

For related but non-causal events, say:

```text
They fit the same broader sentiment backdrop, but that is not the same as a direct causal link.
```
