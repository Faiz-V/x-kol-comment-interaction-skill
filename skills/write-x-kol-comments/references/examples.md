# Output examples

All source scenarios are hypothetical. Use them to calibrate structure and restraint.

## Unverified on-chain chart

**Source:** A post claims active wallets rose sharply during a token rally.

### Best pick

**English:** Assuming the chart is accurate, wallet growth is more interesting than the price move itself. The follow-through will show whether this is sustained usage or short-term momentum chasing.

**中文翻译：** 如果图表准确，钱包增长其实比价格上涨本身更值得关注。后续表现才能说明这是持续使用，还是短期追涨。

**Why this angle:** It preserves uncertainty and separates adoption from price action.

### Option 2 — Concise

**English:** Price gets the headline; repeat usage will decide whether the signal matters.

**中文翻译：** 价格吸引眼球，但重复使用才会决定这个信号是否真正重要。

## AI standards

**Source:** Several labs may adopt a voluntary model-evaluation framework.

### Best pick

**English:** Voluntary standards can look soft on paper, but they often become the rulebook before regulation catches up. The real test is whether this improves safety without locking smaller labs out.

**中文翻译：** 自愿标准看起来约束力不强，但往往会在正式监管跟上之前成为事实规则。真正的考验是，它能否提升安全性，同时不把小型实验室挡在门外。

**Why this angle:** It adds a policy trade-off without claiming the framework has already been adopted.

## Football pressure

**Source:** A young forward scores late after a quiet first half in a hypothetical knockout match.

### Best pick

**English:** Quiet for an hour, decisive when it mattered. Staying mentally present through a difficult match is what turns talent into tournament impact.

**中文翻译：** 前一个小时很安静，但在关键时刻决定了比赛。能在艰难比赛中始终保持专注，正是天赋转化为杯赛影响力的关键。

**Why this angle:** It praises observable match behavior without speculating about private psychology.
